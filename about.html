Our Story

GLOBAL LIFE OUTREACH MINISTRIES was founded with a vision to reach souls for Christ and transform communities through the power of the gospel. We believe that every person deserves an opportunity to experience the love of God and find purpose in Him.

Our ministry is built on the foundations of worship, prayer, and outreach. We strive to create an environment where people can grow spiritually, develop meaningful relationships, and make a positive impact in their communities.
